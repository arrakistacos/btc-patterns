    How the scores work

    Pattern confidence is how textbook the shape is: fit of the swings or trendlines, size relative to recent volatility (ATR), the trend leading into it, volume, and whether it has confirmed with a breakout.

    Hit rate is measured walk-forward on the ~4 days of loaded candles: every time the pattern appeared, did the next candle close in its direction?

    Prediction combines the active patterns, each weighted by its confidence and its hit rate (shrunk toward 50% until it has enough samples). The expected move scales recent average candle size by how lopsided the odds are; the range is ±1 standard deviation of recent 5-minute moves.

    The track record line is the honest scoreboard: if it hovers near 50%, the patterns aren't adding edge right now.

    Data streams straight from the exchange to your browser (WebSocket, with REST resync every minute and automatic failover). Educational tool, not financial advice — 5-minute patterns are noisy, so weigh the hit rates. Charts by TradingView Lightweight Charts.
