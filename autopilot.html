Start practice over with$

Practice uses the real Polymarket US order book and real results with pretend money. It clears practice trades and stats; live history is untouched.

How it works: the signal bot on your laptop places the order the moment it sends a BUY alert, as an immediate-or-cancel limit order at most 1¢ above the best price, and only if the win chance still beats the price plus Polymarket's fee. One trade per 5-minute window. It pauses for an hour after 2 losses in a row (adjustable), and halts with an urgent alert if the money runs out, the daily loss limit is hit, or Polymarket rejects the key. To save phone-alert messages, the bot sends status when you open this page or change something, and otherwise at most every 20 minutes. Not financial advice.
